About Kondapalli Toys

Kondapalli toys are made from soft wood known as Tella Poniki which are found in nearby Kondapalli Hills. The wood is first carved out and then the edges are smooth finished. The later step involves coloring with either oil and water-colors or vegetable dyes and enamel paints are applied based on the type of the toys.

At Krishna Toys, we preserve this 400-year-old tradition by bringing you the finest craftsmanship directly from the artisans of Andhra Pradesh.
